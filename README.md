## 🚀 What I'm Building

### Work Social

A platform where social connection meets real-world work.

- 👥 **Social** — connect and communicate
- 🏠 **Work** — organize real-world work experiences
- 🤝 **Opportunities** — discover work beyond existing personal networks
- 🏢 **Business** — help companies connect with workers and contractors
- 🛍️ **Market Discovery** — help businesses and shops discover products and opportunities beyond their usual circles
- 🌐 **Online + Offline** — designed with real-world connectivity constraints in mind

🔗 **Project:** [Work Social](https://github.com/rasheed113/work-social)

## 🛠️ Engineering Principles

- **Real implementation** — no fake functionality or mock success paths
- **Production first** — build for actual use, not just demos
- **Security & reliability** — permissions, data integrity, and safe boundaries matter
- **Incremental delivery** — build and verify real vertical slices
- **Clear architecture** — keep domains and responsibilities separated
- **Offline-aware thinking** — real-world work should not depend blindly on a perfect connection
